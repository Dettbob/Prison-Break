Turn-based prison break for 1 to 4 prisoners

THREE STRIKES

Sneak, lockpick, bribe and hack your way out before the guards catch you three times. Every prison is built fresh from a seed.

Turns

Each prisoner has 2 action points (AP). When everyone is done, guards and inmates move. Hot-seat: pass the device when asked.

Days and nights

The week repeats: Morning, Day, Evening, Night. Guard patrol routes are the same every Monday morning, every Tuesday night and so on. The Informant sells them (expensive). At night everyone must be in their own cell. Sleep in your cell to skip the night.

Suspicious or dangerous?

  • Yellow ? Suspicious: looting, running, dealing contraband, bribing, being in a restricted room. If caught, the guard searches you and takes contraband. No strike.
  • Red ! Dangerous: out of your cell at night, staying in a restricted room, fighting, attacking guards, being betrayed by an inmate. Caught means a strike.
  • When a guard stops you, you get to react: cooperate, bribe, attack or run.

Strikes

Strike 1: beaten, contraband and your cell stash confiscated, back to your cell. Strike 2: solitary. Strike 3: transferred, you are out. Cigarettes are never confiscated.

Reading the map

  • Red eye on a guard and a red line to you: he sees you right now. Red tint = the tiles he sees while he sees you.
  • Grey slashed eye: a guard in view who does not see you. Blue tint = the tiles he can see. Walls block sight completely.

Difficulty

Easy: fewer guards, the whole map known from the start, and being out of your cell at night is no strike (guards still hunt you). Medium: standard. Hard: more guards. Progress saves automatically.

Hiding

In corridors and yards, guards within 2 tiles may spot you. In rooms like the kitchen only a guard on your tile might. In small closed rooms, only the same tile again with a lower chance. Dark helps.

Gear

  • Salvage scrap, craft lockpicks, shivs, dummies, jammers and wirecutters.
  • Lockpicking most rooms trips the alarm. Use a jammer or bribe a guard to switch alarms off for a time of day.
  • Put a dummy in your bed so empty beds do not trigger a hunt.
  • Meth: 3 AP for 7 turns, then 1 AP for 15 turns.
  • Your cell has a stash. Nobody else can open it.

Inmates

They follow routines, trade, and can be hired to start a brawl in a place and time you choose. Do not steal from someone's cell while he is in it. If they see you do violence they may demand money or betray you.

Getting out

  • Fence: wirecutters, 2 AP, loud.
  • Main gate: a keycard, with no guard watching.
  • Hack the control room to blind cameras or cut the power in the electrical room.

Keys

Arrows or WASD move, Shift sneaks, E ends turn, H hides, F loots, Esc pauses, ? tips. Drag to pan, wheel or pinch to zoom.
